Why it's worth a look

  • Free groceries: earn 10 points per $1 at Loblaw grocery stores, 25 per $1 at Shoppers Drug Mart, and 5 per $1 everywhere else with the PC Money Account. 10,000 points = $10 off at the till.
  • No monthly fee, no minimum balance. Free Interac e-Transfers, free ATM access at 740+ locations, and an optional savings feature paying 2.75% interest.
  • Prefer a credit card? The no-annual-fee PC Mastercard earns 10 points per $1 on groceries, 25 per $1 at Shoppers, and 30+ points per litre at Esso and Mobil — 10 points per $1 everywhere else. PC Financial decides which no-fee tier you get when you apply; higher tiers earn more.
  • Points stack up on things you buy anyway: direct deposits and bill payments can earn points too (up to 5,000 points a month each).

Who it's for

Best if you already shop at Loblaws-family stores or Shoppers — that's where the points add up fastest. Less useful if you rarely shop there, since points are mainly redeemed in those stores.

What my code does — honestly

Using my code doesn't give you an extra bonus. If you're approved for a PC Mastercard or PC Money Account and spend $100 in your first 60 days, I receive 25,000 PC Optimum points (about $25 in groceries). You get the account's own benefits either way — the code doesn't take anything away from you, it just doesn't add anything for you.

Welcome offer

New accounts can currently get a welcome offer on top of the everyday points — $100 in points for a new PC Money Account, or 50,000 points for a new PC Mastercard after spending $100 at participating stores within 60 days of approval (apply by Jan 31, 2027). Offers change by campaign, so check the application page for the current bonus and whether it can be combined with a referral.

Good to know

  • To count as a qualifying referral, you must be new to PC Bank, enter the code in your online application, and spend $100 in the first 60 days (cash advances don't count).
  • One honest note: PC's terms say this code is meant to be shared with friends you've actually talked with, not posted publicly — so I can't promise the referral reward will count when it's used from a public page.
  • Like any credit card, interest applies if you carry a balance — the points only pay off if you pay in full.
